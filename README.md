# Love in Disguise (나의 유죄인간)

> A former special forces officer turned detective cross-dresses as a male secretary and bodyguard to secretly investigate a reclusive chaebol heir suspected of murder.

## Overview
Based on the web novel *Why You Don't Know It's a Girl?* by Lee A-yeon and Ryu Do-ha, **Love in Disguise** is a South Korean workplace romantic comedy and mystery drama airing on tvN and streaming globally on [Amazon Prime Video](https://www.primevideo.com).

### Key Details
* **Original Title:** `나의 유죄인간`
* **Genre:** Workplace Romantic Comedy, Mystery
* **Original Network:** tvN (Broadcast Mondays & Tuesdays at `20:50 KST`)
* **Streaming Platform:** [Amazon Prime Video](https://www.primevideo.com)
* **Source Material:** *Why You Don't Know It's a Girl?*

### Key Plot Highlights
1. High-stakes undercover operation featuring classic cross-dressing comedy and espionage tropes.
2. Unraveling the corporate conspiracy behind the former secretary's murder.
3. Evolving tension and romance inside the executive suites of `Taegang Group`.
